*Zen and the Art of [nothing]* is a book about philosophy, business, and the stories we tell ourselves. It was written by John Zdanowski and Jeff Abrams and published by [CoAuthor.one](https://coauthor.one) in 2024.

It celebrates the fiftieth anniversary of Robert Pirsig's *Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance: An Inquiry into Values* and applies the [Metaphysics of Quality](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/metaphysics-of-quality/) to how we see our lives, our minds, and our businesses.

## What It Is

Fifteen chapters and about twenty poems. The book walks from first principles — what is good? what is a philosophy? — through [how we know what we know](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/sonar-systems/), [the nature of consciousness](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/thinker-and-the-naught/), [how language shapes perception](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/word-is-not-the-thing/), [Pirsig's four levels of quality](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/metaphysics-of-quality/), [how ideas spread](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/adoption-curves/), [the religion of startups](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/startup-religion/), and the [Boot Sequence](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/boot-sequence/) — before arriving at [the story of Weekly Accounting](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/weekly-accounting-story/) and the [Fourth Statement](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/fourth-statement/).

It is [Not-a-Book](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/not-a-book/). The physical copy contains blank pages. The [nothing] in the title is a placeholder for your art.

## What It Is Not

It is not a business manual, though it contains one chapter on business. It is not a self-help book, though it might help. It is not a bible, though it has an [ethos](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/ethos/) and gardening tips in the appendix.

It is book zero of an infinite series.

## Where to Read It

The full text is available free at [weeklyaccounting.com/fullbook](https://weeklyaccounting.com/fullbook).

## The Poems

The book contains [about twenty poems](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/poems/) woven through the chapters — each illustrating a concept in verse. They range from the pointlessness of life in the machine ("[Birth, School, Work, Death](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/poems/birth-school-work-death/)") to the system that replaces it ("[The Intention and the System](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/poems/the-intention-and-the-system/)").
