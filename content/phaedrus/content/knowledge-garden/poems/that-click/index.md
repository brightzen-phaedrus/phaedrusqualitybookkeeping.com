Did you hear that?
That click.
A gear long stuck was freed
To spin another story
To rewrite every creed.

Did you hear that?
That whir.
A fan's long silent slices blur.
Blade cuts through guts and blows the air
The smell of blood is everywhere.

Did you hear that?
That grind.
That freed gear,
It seems, has spun
Another long stuck over here.

Did you see that?
That flash.
The blind went up, a crack of light
Streams through pane
To bathe the blight.

Do you feel that?
That breeze.
Through that broken window
The smoke and pain of war
I'm certain it will never be as it was before.

Did you hear that?
That sigh.
Dusty faces staring
A mother holding baby
Too stunned to even cry.

*From [Zen and the Art of \[nothing\]](https://weeklyaccounting.com/fullbook) by John Zdanowski & Jeff Abrams, Chapter 9.*
