*A poem about the pointlessness of life in the machine.*

Input, process, output.
Food, the use, and waste
Feeds another system,
That must
Consume
In haste.

Delays just make us hungry
And burn what was desire
To try to make a difference
Or just
toss it in
the fire.

Input, process, output.
The Word, your brain, your job.
Feeding you some money,
Your life,
It seems,
They rob.

In truth, you are the giver,
Of time to the machine,
Retirement the vision
Of some
Future life
Serene.

*From [Zen and the Art of \[nothing\]](https://weeklyaccounting.com/fullbook) by John Zdanowski & Jeff Abrams, Chapter 1.*
