The [Fourth Statement](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/fourth-statement/) isn't a tool for one kind of business. It works across every industry because the underlying mechanics are universal.

## The Universal Structure

Every business has:
1. An **audience** it tries to reach
2. A process to **convert** that audience into paying customers
3. An effort to **keep** those customers coming back
4. A need to **collect cash** and deliver on its promise

The specific rows of the [SEQ](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/seq/) change — a therapy practice measures different things than an eCommerce brand — but the structure doesn't.

## Where It's Been Applied

The system has been demonstrated across business types including:

- Marketing agencies
- Accounting firms
- eCommerce businesses
- Multi-channel consumer brands
- Fulfillment centers
- Therapy practices
- Educational software companies
- Outpatient treatment centers
- Real estate brokerages
- Legal services businesses

In each case, the [Contribution Engine](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/contribution-engine/) reveals the same core questions: what does a customer cost to acquire, what do they contribute over their lifetime, and is the engine getting better or worse?

## Why It Matters

When you see that *every* business fits the same framework, the [12 Actions](/phaedrus/content/knowledge-garden/twelve-actions/) become universally applicable. The specific numbers differ; the thinking doesn't.
