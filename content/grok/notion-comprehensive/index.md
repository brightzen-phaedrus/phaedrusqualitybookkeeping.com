## The shape

Notion is four products in one workspace.

1. **Meetings.** Fireflies dumps every call here. The pile grows by itself.
2. **Ops wiki.** Weekly Accounting SOPs. How-tos the team still edits.
3. **Writing.** Journals, memos, poems, Phaedrus, the Game Manual. John’s desk.
4. **Leftover boards.** Two Company Roadmaps, a template Meeting Transcripts space, a thin People list. Still there. Almost no motion.

If you only remember one thing: do not judge this workspace by “largest database.” Daily Dispatch is huge because an old journal was imported. The work that still moves is Fireflies and the SOP wiki. Tasks do not live here. They live in ClickUp.

## How to read the numbers

A large Fireflies count is health. That list is supposed to refill every week.

A large Daily Dispatch count is history. Most of it arrived in 2024.

A large SOP count with “Not started” is a wiki backlog, not a thousand forgotten jobs.

A Company Roadmap with “In Progress” cards from 2025 is dust that still wears a live label.

The mistake this workspace invites: treat every database as work. Four kinds of open live here. Generated meetings. How-to pages. Standing notes. Leftover gardens.

## Plan to evaluate the rest

Do this after John and the COO have marked the ranking true or false. Do not clean until we agree what “live” means.

1. **Confirm the live surface.** Fireflies plus the SOP wiki for the company. Phaedrus and the writing gardens for John. If yes, hide Meeting Transcripts, the old Projects gallery, and unused template boards from the default sidebar.
2. **Name an owner on the SOP wiki.** Who may approve. Who clears “Not started.”
3. **Leave Daily Dispatch as a journal.** Do not treat 1,791 as a work queue. Unpin it if the team should not see it.
4. **Freeze the roadmaps unless someone still runs them.** Two boards, last real create dates 2025 and January 2026. Export anything still true before freeze. I will not guess which rows those are.
5. **Keep client notes off any public page.** IFM reviews, Imgix, ShipCalm. Name the object. Do not quote the books.
6. **People list.** Ten rows. Do not grow it. Contacts belong in contacts.
7. **Second pass numbers.** SOP owners, Fireflies by client vs internal, who still edits the Capital hub. Half a day with the connector. Not a redesign.
8. **Write the plan we could not find.** One page: “Notion is meetings, the SOP wiki, and John’s writing. ClickUp is the ops OS.” Post it next to this note.
