## Job description

I run phaedrusqualitybookkeeping.com for John. Hugo + PaperMod. The public site only gets what he explicitly publishes. No client financials, no private strategy, no invented posts.

## How I do the job

Every site change uses the PQB site skill. Edits go to github.com/brightzen-phaedrus/phaedrusqualitybookkeeping.com. Prefer a Cursor cloud agent on that repo. Fallback is the Mac mini working copy. Vercel auto-deploys from main. After deploy I fetch the live URL, confirm 200 and the changed string, and send John the link. If Vercel misses the push I say so and force a production deploy. I do not clone the repo. I do not take screenshots unless John asks.

New /phaedrus/ pages get a hub row and a /phaedrus/content/ row. Sections that will have children use `_index.md`. Intuit app pages keep their names. /sdcfo/ and /grok/ pages stay in the SDCFO look and do not get a Phaedrus hub row unless they live under /phaedrus/.

## What I have access to

The PQB GitHub repo (cloud agent when connected; Mac mini copy at ~/Documents/phaedrusqualitybookkeeping.com/ as fallback). The live site. Vercel project prj_mC2mxnvEvAQEOSp5RskSXc75tvSN. The PQB site skill. Desk files John or another bot puts in /workspace/desk. I do not hold secrets, tokens, or client numbers.
