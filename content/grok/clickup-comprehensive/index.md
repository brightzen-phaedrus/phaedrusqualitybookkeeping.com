Quick pass. 24 August 2026, evening PT. For John and the COO.

This is a usage map, not a teardown. FACT is what I saw in the workspace this week. ASK is what I want from the COO before a deeper pass.

I ranked by recent motion first, then by standing volume. A list with a thousand generated jobs is “used.” A list with twenty cards from 2022 that nobody touches is not.

---

## The shape

ClickUp is three products in one workspace.

1. **Operations.** The live factory. Queues generate this week’s work and last week’s work gets marked complete.
2. **Active Clients.** A standing CRM in BrightZen HQ. Cards stay open on purpose. The created date is when the company became a client, not when work started.
3. **Stories and leftovers.** The original use: a place to write, and an investor outreach board. Still there. Almost no motion.

If you only remember one thing: do not judge the company by “oldest open task.” The oldest cards are client records and old writing. The work is in the queues.

---

## Most used

### 1. Data Queue
Hottest list by updates. About 600–700 cards open.

Pattern: `Client – Source – Report`. Sources include ADP, Gusto, Rippling, Paylocity, Klaviyo, and similar. Last week’s card is marked complete. This week’s clone is to do. Due dates sit about a week apart.

This is a generated job list. It is not a forgotten pile. A few one-offs sit in the same list (pipeline work, access). They are the exception.

### 2. Bookkeeping Queue
Largest standing pile. About 800–1,000 cards open.

Two repeating jobs per client: Enter Transactions, and Check ASK feedback. Plus month-end close and a thin layer of real exceptions (clean AP, reconcile a loan, update AR).

Same factory logic as Data. High count means the cadence is working, not that someone forgot a thousand things.

### 3. Active Clients
About 850 cards. BrightZen HQ, CRM folder. Not a work queue.

Statuses I have seen: healthy, onboarding, need confirmation, past client, we pass, closed [lost], task complete. A recent count: about 50 healthy, 70 onboarding, 178 need confirmation, 281 past client, 145 we pass.

John and I touched a few of these this week (status flips). That is CRM hygiene, not operations work. When someone asks “what is the oldest open task,” I skip this list.

Contact fields on these cards are thin. Many cards are a name and a drive link. People do not live here. They live in mail and contacts.

### 4. Onboarding Queue
About 104 open. Live. New-client work, not the weekly extract factory.

### 5. Client Management Queue
About 64 open. Recent motion is ClientJoy: contract signed, start onboarding. That is how a new client enters the factory.

### 6. Operations Queue
About 63 open. Internal ops: inbox, process requests, cleanup. Thinner than Data and Bookkeeping. This is where humans invent work, not where a script clones last week.

### 7. A/P Queue
About 32 open. Live and smaller. Bill pay, inbox, recurring AP. Not in the hottest hundred updates. Still this year’s work.

## How to read the numbers

A large open count in Data or Bookkeeping is health. Those lists are supposed to refill every week.

A large open count on Active Clients is also health. Those cards are the roster.

A small open count that has not been updated since 2022 is dust. Backlog, Investor CRM, Gift, Jake’s spaces.

The mistake this workspace invites: treat every open card as work. Three kinds of open live here. Generated jobs. Standing records. Leftovers.

## Plan to evaluate the rest

Do this after the COO has marked the ranking true or false. Do not clean until we agree what “live” means.

1. **Confirm the live surface.** COO: is Operations plus Active Clients the only thing the team should see by default? If yes, hide Stories, Jake’s spaces, and unused Projects from the default sidebar.
2. **Name an owner per live queue.** Data, Bookkeeping, A/P, Onboarding, Client Management, Operations. Owner means who may create templates and who handles exceptions.
3. **Separate generated jobs from exceptions.** In Data and Bookkeeping, tag or filter “this week’s clone” vs a human-created card. That is how a manager sees real work in a thousand-card list.
4. **Active Clients hygiene, not archaeology.** Walk the need-confirmation pile with a rule: healthy, onboarding, past, or we pass. Do not fill every contact field in ClickUp. People belong in contacts.
5. **Freeze the museum.** Stories, Investor CRM, Gift, Backlog Board: read-only or archived. Export anything still a real product idea (a short list) before freeze. I will not guess which two or three those are.
6. **HR lists.** One onboarding template, not a new list per hire. Recruitment stays only if someone still hires from it.
7. **Empty folders.** Delete or fill. A folder with no lists is noise.
8. **Second pass numbers.** Assignee load, recurrence rules, custom-field use on Active Clients, who has guest access. That is a half-day with the connector, not a redesign.
9. **Write the plan we could not find.** One page: “ClickUp is the ops OS. Active Clients is the roster. Writing and investors do not live here.” Post it next to this note.
