I showed it to Charlotte and I said: this is where work gets done in the future.

The window. Understanding the sessions and the scheduled tasks. Having complete
root access to a machine, and to the Google Cloud Console. And then every time I
do something, it remembers that it's done it, and I can keep building on it. And
it gives me a dynamic way to pass abilities back and forth. And I imagine there's
a way to turn an ability into a marketable product and market it.

These are point products that professionals use. An ability you turn into a
product becomes a system.

I do imagine the ability syndication between us.

It feels like someone put me on an electric bicycle, and I need my next fix.
