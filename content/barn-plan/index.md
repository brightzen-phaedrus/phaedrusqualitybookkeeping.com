## 🌿 Plants — Move Away From Walls

**Outdoor plants along barn walls need to be moved at least 10 feet out.** The fumigation tent drapes over the structure and the gas can affect nearby vegetation.

- [ ] Walk the perimeter and identify every plant near the barn walls
- [ ] Potted plants on porches/decks → move to a safe distance
- [ ] Ground-planted shrubs/bushes touching walls → cut back if possible
- [ ] Water everything deeply before moving — they won't get watered for 2–3 days
- [ ] Cover any shade-loving plants if their temporary spot is in full sun
- [ ] All indoor plants → remove from the barn entirely

**Timeline:** Start moving plants **June 27 or 28** — don't wait until the morning of.

## 📦 What Must Be Removed From the Barn

The fumigation gas (Vikane / sulfuryl fluoride) penetrates everything. Anything you eat, drink, breathe, or put on your body needs to come out or be sealed in Nylofume bags.

### Kitchen — EVERYTHING
- [ ] All food from the refrigerator and freezer
- [ ] All food from pantry and shelves
- [ ] Spices, oils, vinegars, condiments
- [ ] Produce on counters
- [ ] Bread, snacks, cereal — anything open or in thin packaging
- [ ] Canned goods (if not double-sealed in Nylofume bags)
- [ ] Coffee, tea, sugar
- [ ] Pet food and pet treats
- [ ] Bottled water and drinks
- [ ] Baby food (if applicable)
- [ ] Wine, beer, spirits (sealed bottles can stay if double-bagged, but safest to remove)

### Bathroom & Medicine
- [ ] All prescription medications
- [ ] Over-the-counter meds (ibuprofen, allergy meds, etc.)
- [ ] Vitamins and supplements
- [ ] Toothpaste, mouthwash
- [ ] Lotions, creams, sunscreen
- [ ] Shampoo and conditioner (if bottles are open)
- [ ] Contact lens solution
- [ ] First aid supplies

### Miscellaneous
- [ ] Candles (wax absorbs the gas)
- [ ] Incense
- [ ] Aerosol cans
- [ ] Rubber items (can be damaged)
- [ ] Crayons, Play-Doh, art supplies
- [ ] Tobacco products
- [ ] Any open containers of cleaning products
