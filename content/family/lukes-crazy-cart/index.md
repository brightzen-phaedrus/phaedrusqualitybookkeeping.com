Luke has a standard Razor Crazy Cart (black frame / DLX). Stock is a 24V 250W motor and a 24V 5Ah battery. This page is the upgrade path. Prices are from [Taxi Garage](https://taxi-garage.com/collections/standard-crazy-cart-parts) and Amazon on August 26, 2026.

This does not fit XL. It does not fit a V1 red-frame cart with batteries behind the seat.

## What it has now

Taxi Garage replacements: about $50 for the motor, $40 for the battery. The charger is $7. The controller is $20.

## The upgrade ladder

You cannot drop a hotter motor onto the stock 24V pack. Each step needs a matching battery.

**36V lithium battery, $300.** Plug and play. The first real upgrade. Faster than stock, about 1.5–2 hours of run time. Reviews put it near 15 mph. It is also the battery the 500W motor expects later.

**500W 36V motor, $500.** Needs the $300 battery. About $800 all-in. Taxi Garage voids the warranty over 150 lb, or if you skip their battery.

**900W 48V motor, $620.** Up to 28 mph at 165 lb. The 48V battery is a dropdown add-on. There is no standalone 48V battery for a standard cart. The 48V pack listed separately is for XL. Warranty void over 220 lb.

**1100W 48V complete kit, $900.** Includes the 48V battery. Up to 30 mph at 165 lb. Smoother takeoff than the 900W. Reuse the stock front axle, spacers, and wheel.

The 900W and 1100W kits are a different machine. They need a Taxi Garage neck and real brakes. The hydraulic brake kit is $440 if you already have a TG neck, $700 with one. It will not bolt to the stock fork.

## What to buy first

If the goal is more cart and still a kid cart, buy the 36V battery. It is the only upgrade that is truly plug and play on the stock motor.

## Amazon

Amazon has no real upgrade kit for the standard cart. Taxi Garage does not sell there.

Stock motor is about $53. Batteries run $29–$56. A 24V 500W motor is $100 with weak reviews. A stock restore on Amazon is about $80–$110. A real power bump is still Taxi Garage.

Skip 36V / XL motors and 48–72V go-kart kits in that search. They do not fit this cart.


## Speed and battery life

Stock is 12 mph and about 40 minutes (Razor, 24V 250W, 24V 5Ah lead-acid).

The 36V lithium battery alone, same 250W motor, is 14–15+ mph and about 75 minutes to 2 hours. Taxi Garage says this raises both speed and ride time. It is the first real upgrade. The battery is 12Ah, 36V, about $300. About 3.5 times the energy of the stock pack. They claim 2 to 3 times the ride.

A 36V 500W motor is 18–20+ mph (from TG Stage 2/3 complete carts at about 192 lb; the bare $500 kit page has no mph). It needs the 36V battery. About $800 all-in. Warranty void over 150 lb.

48V 900W is 26–28 mph (28 at 165 lb on the kit page, 26+ at 192 lb on Stage 4). 48V 1100W is about 30 mph at 165 lb. Both kits claim 1.5–2 hours. Taxi Garage never published Ah for the standard-cart 48V pack. The 1100W kit ($900) includes the 48V battery. There is no standalone 48V battery for the standard cart.

Heavier rider, slower cart. Stock max is 200 lb. Over 200 lb they tell you to skip the battery-only step and go 500W+.
