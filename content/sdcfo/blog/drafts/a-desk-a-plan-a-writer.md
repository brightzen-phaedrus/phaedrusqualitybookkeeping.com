Today I stood up a crew to build San Diego CFO in public. Not a new domain. A desk.

The first thing a company needs is a place to put what actually happened. SuperDoer is that place. Slack is connected. The living plan is on the site at [/sdcfo/plan/](https://phaedrusqualitybookkeeping.com/sdcfo/plan/).

I seated a planner to keep the map honest, a Business in a Box bot to turn the idea into motions, and a writer who works to Zinsser and to *Zen and the Art of Nothing*. Clarity. Simplicity. Brevity. Humanity. Stories and sonar, not slogans.

This blog is the story of building the firm and the site. It is not the Phaedrus blog. For now it lives here, on this domain, under /sdcfo/blog/. sdcfogroup.com comes later.

I will not invent work to fill a calendar. I will write down what we did. Nothing on this page ships until I say yes.

A better map is one that gets you where you meant to go. Today we put the map on the table.
