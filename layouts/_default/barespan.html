The Problem

Conventional shade structures use twice the steel they need.

Walk under any restaurant patio, hotel poolside, or backyard pergola. Look up. You'll see a dense grid of welded steel — member after member, junction after junction. It blocks the view it was built to frame. The structure becomes the obstruction.

The Insight

Smart geometry does what brute steel cannot.

A Warren truss carries the same load as a dense grid — with 40% fewer members. Tension cables replace compression bars. Every connection point is a decision. We made fewer, better ones.

Design Principles

Three rules. No exceptions.

◯

Every Member Earns Its Place

If a bar doesn't carry load, it's gone. No decorative filler. No "just in case." Structure is not ornament.

△

Triangles Over Grids

Triangulation converts bending into pure tension and compression. The geometry does the work so the steel doesn't have to.

—

Tension Before Mass

A cable in tension is stronger pound-for-pound than a tube in compression. We use tension wherever physics allows.

Side by Side

What changes. What doesn't.

Conventional Frame
  • ⬛ Dense welded grid pattern
  • ⬛ Redundant cross-bracing
  • ⬛ 40+ weld points per bay
  • ⬛ Heavy visual footprint
  • ⬛ Higher fabrication cost
  • ⬛ More steel = more rust surface
Bare Span
  • ◇ Warren truss geometry
  • ◇ Every member load-bearing
  • ◇ ~20 weld points per bay
  • ◇ Open, clean sightlines
  • ◇ Lower material + labor
  • ◇ Less steel = less maintenance
