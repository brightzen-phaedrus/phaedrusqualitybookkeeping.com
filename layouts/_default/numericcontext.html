{{/* ── Section 2: The Card ── */}}
How a card lives — the heart of it

The conversation stays with the data.

A metric matters this week. The person responsible highlights it on the dashboard. That single act produces a card — the chart, the marked data point, and their commentary on why it moved. The card pushes to Slack and the company feed. Anyone following the right hashtag sees it. Next week's card sits on top of last week's conversation. Institutional memory as a byproduct of normal work, not a documentation chore.

{{/* ── Section 3: Sections vs. Hashtags ── */}}
Two axes that keep the system legible

Sections organize the source. Hashtags organize the reader.

Sections are organizational — slices of the dashboard by department, effectively separate operational views for how the work is divided. Hashtags are attentional — each person curates their own set and follows only the metrics they care about. The CFO follows #cash and #margin. The head of marketing follows #funnel and #cac. Same data, different feeds. Structure at the source, personalization at the reader.

Two different axes, both essential.
