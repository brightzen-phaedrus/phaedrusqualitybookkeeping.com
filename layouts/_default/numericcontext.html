Your Numbers, Your Call

Anonymization is built in. Both layers.

The course shows real businesses. None of them are exposed unless they want to be. Two independent toggles let you share the shape of your business without revealing the size — or share nothing at all.

Your company name never appears in any course material, anonymized case study, or comparative analysis. The shape of your business can be discussed without the label.

🔢 Number Anonymization

Your actual dollar figures can be scaled, scrubbed, or indexed before any external view. The patterns come through. The amounts stay private.

You can also opt out of being included entirely. Default is private. Sharing is always a choice.
