{{/* ── The Card ── */}}

Highlight a number. The story starts there.

A metric moves. The person responsible highlights it and says why. That's a post — the chart, the data point, their commentary. It shows up in the feed and pushes to Slack. Next week's post sits on top of this week's conversation.

{{/* ── Highlight / Follow / Remember ── */}}

One rhythm for every metric that matters

Highlight. Follow. Remember.

Highlight

Mark what moved.

The person closest to the number says what happened and why. One click creates the post.

Follow

See what you care about.

Hashtags let each person follow only the metrics that matter to their role.

Remember

The thread never scrolls away.

Commentary stays attached to the number. Institutional memory as a byproduct, not a chore.

{{/* ── The Wedge ── */}}

Where every dashboard stops

Dashboards show numbers. Numeric Context tells their story.

Every analytics tool stops at visibility. The interpretation happens in a meeting that doesn't get recorded. A Slack thread that scrolls away. Numeric Context keeps the who, the why, and the what-happened-next with the number itself.
